# EZTO Whitepaper

**From Meme Coin to a Community Trading Platform on Solana**

Version 2.0 (draft): subject to change

---

## 1. Introduction

### What is EZTO?

EZTO is a community token on the **Solana** blockchain. It starts as a fun, open meme coin and grows step by step into a set of real products: a simple swap interface, staking, and payments.

Many meme coins live only on hype. EZTO's goal is to keep the fun of a meme coin and add real, usable products over time, built in public with code that anyone can read.

### Why Solana?

- **Fast:** transactions confirm in seconds.
- **Cheap:** fees are usually a fraction of a cent.
- **Big ecosystem:** wallets (Phantom, Solflare), DEXs (Raydium, Orca) and aggregators (Jupiter) are mature and widely used.

## 2. Vision & Mission

### Vision

To make crypto trading simple for everyone, with EZTO at the center of an easy, non-custodial trading experience.

### Mission

To build simple and transparent tools on Solana for swapping, holding and using digital assets, while users keep control of their own funds.

### Core Values

- ✅ **Simplicity:** easy for beginners.
- ✅ **Transparency:** open-source code, public wallets, public token unlock schedules.
- ✅ **Security:** no mint authority, no freeze authority, locked liquidity.
- ✅ **Decentralization:** non-custodial products, so users always hold their own keys.
- ✅ **Community:** the project grows with its holders.

## 3. A Clean, Fair Token

EZTO is a **clean token**. It has:

| Property | EZTO |
|---|---|
| Buy / sell tax | **0%**: no hidden fees |
| Mint authority | **Revoked**: no one can ever create more EZTO |
| Freeze authority | **Revoked**: no one can freeze your tokens |
| Liquidity | **LP tokens burned**: liquidity cannot be pulled |
| Team tokens | **Locked & vested**: on-chain, publicly verifiable |
| Code | **Open source** |

Anyone can verify these properties on a Solana explorer (Solscan, Solana Explorer) using the token's mint address.

| Allocation | % | Amount | Release |

| Platform Development | 20% | 200,000,000 | Locked: 6-month cliff, then released linearly over 24 months |
| Marketing & Listings | 15% | 150,000,000 | 5% unlocked at launch for listings & campaigns; remaining 10% released linearly over 12 months |

All locks will be created using an established on-chain vesting protocol (such as Streamflow), and the contract links will be published so anyone can verify them.

### Utility

- **Trading pair:** EZTO is the main token of the EZTO swap platform.
- **Fee discounts:** holders pay lower fees on the EZTO platform.
- **Staking rewards:** stake EZTO to earn rewards from the Staking & Rewards pool.
- **Payments:** pay in online stores that accept EZTO (future integration).

## 5. Roadmap

Dates will be announced as each phase is ready. We will deliver phase by phase and share progress openly.

### Phase 1: Launch 🚀
- Create the EZTO token with mint & freeze authority revoked.
- Publish the website, whitepaper and open-source code.
- Launch the liquidity pool on a Solana DEX (Raydium) and burn the LP tokens.
- Lock team, platform and reserve tokens on-chain.
- Build the community (X / Twitter, Telegram).

### Phase 2: Growth 🔥
- Get listed on trackers (CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap).
- Run community campaigns and partnerships.
- Launch the **EZTO staking** program.
- Apply for centralized exchange (CEX) listings.

### Phase 3: EZTO Swap Platform 💱
- Launch a **non-custodial swap interface**: users trade directly from their own wallet, routed through Solana's best liquidity (e.g. the Jupiter aggregator).
- Fee discounts for EZTO holders.
- Simple portfolio and price tools.

### Phase 4: Ecosystem 🌐
- Payment integrations for online stores.
- Advanced trading tools.
- Community governance on selected decisions.
