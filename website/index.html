FAQ

Questions, answered.

Has EZTO launched yet?

Not yet. The official contract address will be published on this website and pinned in our Telegram and on X. Until then, any token called EZTO is not ours.

How do I stay safe from scams?

Only trust the contract address shown on this website. Our team will never DM you first, ask for your seed phrase, or ask you to send funds.

Is there a buy or sell tax?

No. EZTO has 0% tax on every buy, sell and transfer.

Can more EZTO ever be created?

No. The mint authority is revoked right after launch, so the supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 forever. Anyone can verify this on a Solana explorer.

Who is behind EZTO?

EZTO is led by an independent founder and built in public with open-source code. The team will grow with contributors from the community.

What will EZTO be used for?

EZTO is the core token of the planned EZTO Swap platform (fee discounts), the staking program, and future payment integrations.
