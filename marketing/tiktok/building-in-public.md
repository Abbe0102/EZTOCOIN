# TikTok Series: "Building EZTO in public"

A 10-episode series that turns the build itself into content. People follow stories, so every episode shows real progress and ends with one clear action: **join the Telegram**.

## Format rules (every episode)
- **Length:** 15–35 s. Vertical 9:16. Filmed on phone is fine.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds:** big on-screen text + the episode number ("Day 3 of building a meme coin the honest way").
- **On-screen captions** for everything you say (most people watch muted).
- **One idea per video.**
- **End card (last 2–3 s):** "Join our Telegram 👉 link in bio" over the EZTO logo.
- **Never** mention price, profit or "100x". Show work, not promises.
- **Pin a comment** on every video: `Website: eztocoin.com · Telegram: link in bio · EZTO has not launched yet, any EZTO token today is fake.`

**Caption template**
```
Building EZTO in public, episode [N]: [title] ⚡ Vision first, hype later.
#EZTO #Solana #crypto #buildinpublic #memecoin
```

**Posting rhythm:** 3–4 episodes per week. Best times to test: 12:00–14:00 and 19:00–22:00 local time. Keep what works.

**Useful phone tools:** CapCut (editing, auto-captions), screen recording (iPhone Control Center), the images in `marketing/posts/` and `brand/`.
